Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · updated for email previews and actions

Mail Counter is a Chrome extension that shows how many unread conversations are in your Gmail Inbox. This page explains what data it uses and what happens to it. In short: it reads as little as possible, keeps it in your browser, and sends it nowhere except back to Google.

Data the extension accesses

When you connect a Google account, with your permission, Mail Counter accesses:

  • Your email address, through the openid and email scopes. It is used only to label the account in the extension and to make sure a token belongs to the right account.
  • Your Gmail, through the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify scope. Mail Counter uses it to:
    • read how many unread conversations are in your Inbox;
    • read the sender, subject, date and short snippet of up to 25 unread Inbox conversations per account, to list them in the popup;
    • read the body of one email when you open its preview;
    • mark a conversation as read, archive it, or move it to spam, only when you click that button.
    It never sends email, never deletes mail (the permission can't delete permanently), and never reads conversations that are already read, other folders, or attachments.

How the data is stored

  • Access tokens from Google are stored in your browser's local extension storage on that device. They expire after about an hour and are replaced automatically.
  • Unread counts and previews (sender, subject, date and snippet of unread Inbox conversations) and the time of the last check are stored in the same local storage so the popup opens instantly. A conversation's preview is removed as soon as it's no longer unread.
  • Email bodies are fetched only when you open a preview and are kept in memory while the popup is open. They are never written to storage. Remote images in an email are blocked unless you click Show images.
  • The list of connected email addresses is stored in Chrome's extension sync storage. If you use Chrome Sync, Chrome syncs it to your other devices through your Google account, so the extension knows which accounts to check there.
  • Your options (how often each account is checked, and whether the chime plays) are stored in the same sync storage.

Sharing

Mail Counter has no server. The developer receives none of your data. The extension talks only to Google: accounts.google.com to get access tokens, and gmail.googleapis.com and openidconnect.googleapis.com for your mail and email address. The only other requests are images inside an email, and only after you click Show images. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking, and no data is sold, transferred or shared with anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Mail Counter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • To disconnect an account, right-click the extension's icon, choose Options, and click Remove next to it. That deletes its token, stored count, previews and options.
  • To revoke Mail Counter's access entirely, go to your Google Account's third-party connections.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored in your browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. The full history is in the source repository.
