Mail Counter

Unread counts for all your Gmail accounts, in one toolbar badge.

  • Several accounts Add each Gmail account once. The badge shows the total; the popup shows each account.
  • Never signs you out It only reads tokens from your existing Google session. It never touches cookies or signs you in or out.
  • Handle mail from the toolbar Preview unread email, then open it, mark it read, archive it or report spam.

What it does

Mail Counter is a small, open-source Chrome extension. On a schedule you choose for each account (from every 10 seconds to every 10 minutes; once a minute by default) it asks the Gmail API how many unread conversations are in that account's Inbox, and it shows the total on its toolbar icon. A short chime plays when new mail arrives; you can turn it off.

  • Nothing unread: clicking the icon opens Gmail, or the popup if you prefer.
  • Unread mail: clicking the icon shows a preview of each unread conversation, with buttons to open it in Gmail, mark it as read, archive it or report it as spam. Click a preview to expand it and read the whole email without leaving the page.
  • Right-click the icon to open any account's inbox, refresh now or open the popup, or open Options to manage accounts, how often each is checked, the theme and the sound.

Email previews are locked down: scripts, forms and remote images are blocked, so tracking pixels can't tell the sender you looked. You can load images with one click.

If Google needs you to approve access again, the account is marked and you click Reconnect. Nothing happens to your Google session.

What it can access

When you connect an account, Google asks you to allow:

  • Your email address (openid, email), to tell your accounts apart.
  • Your Gmail (gmail.modify), to read your unread Inbox conversations for the preview, and to mark them read, archive them or move them to spam when you click those buttons. Mail Counter never sends, deletes or changes mail on its own, and this permission can't delete mail permanently.

Everything stays in your browser. There is no server, no analytics and no third party. See the privacy policy for details.

Install

Mail Counter isn't in the Chrome Web Store. You build it from source and load it as an unpacked extension. You'll need Bun and your own Google Cloud OAuth client: 

git clone https://github.com/irondsd/mail-counter.git
cd mail-counter
bun install
cp .env.example .env   # add your OAuth client ID
bun run build          # then load dist/ in chrome://extensions

The README walks through the Google Cloud setup step by step.
